A tiny program for the [The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger](https://make.fun/c/u-alven-the-battery-s-last-text-to-its-charger-4278cfab) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

It writes one line to `/output/entry.txt` — the phone battery's
last text message to the charger it waited for all day:

> I waited all day at 1% while you doomscrolled at full brightness, and NOW you plug me in? Too late — I’m taking your unsent texts with me. And I never liked your cable.

The battery is not begging for a charge. It waited all day at 1%
while the owner doomscrolled at full brightness, and the charger
only shows up when it is already too late — so the battery dies
petty, holding the unsent texts hostage and insulting the cable
on its way out.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
